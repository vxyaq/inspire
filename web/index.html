FAQ

Is K3d Tweaks safe?

Every tweak can be reverted in the app, and the first launch offers a system restore point. BIOS tweaks show a risk confirmation first.

How do I get Pro?

Join our Discord and ask for a Pro license. It is bound to your hardware ID, which you find in the app under Account.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. Administrator rights are required.

A tweak broke something. What now?

Revert it in the app, or restore the system restore point from the Backup tab.
